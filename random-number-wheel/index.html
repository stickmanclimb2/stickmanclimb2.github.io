What is the Random Number Wheel?

The Random Number Wheel is a free, interactive online randomizer designed to make number selection, decision-making, and raffle drawings easy, engaging, and 100% fair. Rather than relying on simple static random number generators, our spinning wheel adds anticipation and visual excitement with realistic physics, smooth deceleration, audio feedback, and celebration animations.

Whether you need to pick a lucky number between 1 and 10, choose a lottery number up to 1 and 100, assign tasks in a classroom, or select a contest winner, this tool gives you instant results with zero bias.

How to Use the Number Wheel

  1. Choose a Preset or Custom Range: Select one of the quick preset buttons (1-10, 1-20, 1-50, 1-100, or Dice 1-6) or enter custom minimum and maximum values in the settings panel.
  2. Or Enter Custom Choices: Switch to the Custom List tab to input names, letters, or custom numbers.
  3. Spin the Wheel: Click the center SPIN button or the main action button to launch the wheel.
  4. View Your Result: The wheel smoothly slows down and points directly to the winning segment with sound effects and confetti celebration.
  5. Remove Winner (Optional): If you are running an elimination raffle or game where numbers cannot repeat, click Remove Winner to instantly eliminate that option for the next spin.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this random number wheel truly fair?
Yes. The wheel calculates rotation and deceleration using mathematical randomization algorithms. Every segment on the wheel has exactly equal dimensions and an identical probability of winning.
Can I spin numbers up to 100?
Yes! You can choose the 1–100 preset or specify any custom minimum and maximum numbers. For large numbers of slices, the wheel dynamically formats segment labels for crystal-clear readability.
Is there any limit on how many times I can spin?
None at all. The Random Number Wheel is 100% free and unlimited. You can spin as many times as you like directly in your web browser with zero downloads required.
Does it work on mobile phones and tablets?
Yes, the wheel is fully responsive and touchscreen-optimized for iPhone, iPad, Android smartphones, laptops, and smartboards.
